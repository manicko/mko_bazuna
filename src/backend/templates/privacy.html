Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mko Bazuna collects, processes, and protects your data, including the cookies we set and the third parties we share data with. Last updated: August 2026.

1. Controller

The data controller for this service is the operator of the Mko Bazuna classifieds board. You can contact us over Telegram at @{{ bot_username }}.

2. Processing Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Service delivery and authentication — legal basis: performance of a contract / legitimate interest.
  • Seller-buyer contact relay via Telegram deep-links — legal basis: your explicit initiation.
  • Traffic analytics via Plausible (anonymized) — legal basis: your consent (analytics category).
  • Personalization (language, city) — legal basis: your consent (preferences category).
  • Language normalization of ad text via Google Translate — legal basis: best-effort, non-identifying content transfer.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

  • Telegram — authentication and seller-buyer contact relay via deep-links (t.me/{{ bot_username }}). No ad content is shared; only the identifiers needed for the action.
  • Google Translate — best-effort, non-identifying language normalization of ad text. No personal data (telegram_id, username, IP) is transmitted.
  • Plausible Analytics — cookieless, privacy-friendly traffic analytics. Loaded only after you accept the analytics category.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate personal data.
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

6. Erasure and Retention

When you withdraw consent, your account and its advertisements are soft-deleted immediately. All personal data is permanently erased within 30 days of withdrawal. Consent state is otherwise retained for 12 months, after which you are re-prompted.

7. Manage Your Consent

You can revisit and change your consent choices at any time. Use the Cookie settings link to open the consent banner, or contact us on Telegram at @{{ bot_username }}.
